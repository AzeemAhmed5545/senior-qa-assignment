## Method and limits

- Name and price compared after collapsing whitespace and decoding HTML entities; price compared as
  a number (Rs. 1,000 = 1000).
- Catalogue values come from the product cards on /products; product page values from the product
  information block; main image is the image in the product view block.
- 'Contains the product name' is a factual string check; whether wording is _useful_ is listed under
  Content to review.
- One request at a time with a short pause; failed requests are retried twice and then reported
  under Checks not completed.
- Only server HTML is checked (no JavaScript run). Content added by scripts after load is not
  covered.
